Thank you for considering taking part. Please read this before we start. You can
ask questions at any point.

## What this is

We're asking you to talk with us about how you plan and keep track of your days,
and possibly to try Evidence of Life, a planner and daily recap web app. The
session takes about 30 minutes. This is product research, **not** a medical or
clinical study.

## What we will and won't ask

- We will ask about everyday routines, planning, and tools you've used.
- **We will not ask about any diagnosis, medication, or health condition.** If
  you mention one, we won't write it down.
- You can skip any question without giving a reason.

## What we record

Only what you agree to below:

- [ ] Written notes taken by the researcher (always, unless you say no)
- [ ] Audio recording
- [ ] Screen recording while you use the app
- [ ] Short quotes of what you say, used in internal notes or public summaries
      with no name attached

If you create an account during the session, it is a normal account. You can ask
us to delete it, and the data in it, afterwards.

## How we protect your privacy

- Notes use a **pseudonym** (for example "P03"), never your name or email.
- **Recordings are never committed to the project's code repository** and are
  never published. They are kept privately and deleted once we've written up
  our notes.

- Any public summary (for example a GitHub issue) contains only anonymous
  observations, never anything that could identify you.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary.
- **You can stop at any time**, during or after the session, without giving a
  reason. If you stop, we will delete your recordings and notes on request.
- You can ask us what we recorded about you.
