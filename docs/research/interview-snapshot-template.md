# Interview snapshot (one per session)

Fill this in within an hour of the session, while it's fresh. Copy the file to a
private location; **do not commit filled-in snapshots** if they contain anything
that could identify a person. Use pseudonyms only, and never record diagnosis,
medication, or health details.

---

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Participant ID (pseudonym)** | e.g. P01 |
| **Date** | YYYY-MM-DD |
| **Session type** | Problem interview (Part A) / Usability session (Part B) |
| **Moderator / note-taker** | |
| **Consent confirmed** | Yes / No (recording: Yes / No; quotes: Yes / No) |
| **Context** | Rough situation only, e.g. "full-time student, plans on phone". Device and browser used. |

## Observed behaviour

What they actually did, step by step. Note where they paused, backtracked, or
asked for help. Separate what you saw from what you think it means.

-
-

## Quotes

Verbatim, and only if they agreed to quotes being kept. Mark anything
paraphrased as a paraphrase.

-
-

## Pains

Problems they described or showed, in their terms, not ours.

-

## Workarounds

What they do today instead (tools, tricks, habits), including things they tried
and dropped.

-

## Surprises

Anything that contradicted what we assumed about users or about the product.
